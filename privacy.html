Privacy Policy

Effective May 1, 2026

Throttle respects your privacy. We don't sell leads. We don't share your contact info with dealers unless you message them through the platform.

What we collect

If you create an account, we store your name, email, hashed password, and any preferences you set. If you list a vehicle, we store the listing details. We log basic technical data (IP, user agent) for fraud prevention and analytics.

What we share

We never sell your contact information. When you message a dealer, your message and email are visible to that dealer only. We use third-party processors for hosting, email, payments, and analytics — each bound by data-protection terms.

Cookies

We use cookies for authentication, preferences (theme, saved searches), and aggregated analytics. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, export your listings, or delete your account at any time from Settings. EU/CA residents have additional rights under GDPR/CCPA — email privacy@throttle.toys.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@throttle.toys.
